About AXONTEC

PROTECTION THAT STARTS WITH THE RIGHT SYSTEM.

AXONTEC Safety Systems L.L.C. is a Dubai-based fire and life safety solutions provider, professionally managed by engineers with experience in fire detection and fire protection systems.

The company provides fire safety services across system installation, fit-out works, projects and annual maintenance. Its solutions cover a range of building requirements, from fire alarms and sprinklers to suppression systems and emergency lighting.
